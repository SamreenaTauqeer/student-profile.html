My Favourite Chocolate Chip Cookies Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter
  • 1cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2cups maida
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 2 Tsp hot water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 cups chocolate chips

    • Instructions

    Preheat an oven or stove to 175 degree celsius / 350 degree fahrenheit . Now mix butter and sugar finely in a bowl

    beat both the eggs one by one in the same bowl and then add vanilla and make it a batter
    Dissolve baking soda in hot water and add it in the batter along with salt .Stir in flour and chocolate chips.Now fill a table spoon with the batter and add it on a greased baking sheet . Bake for about 10 minutes in oven and if on the stove cook it til gloden brown .
