About Our School

Green Valley High School has been serving students in our community for over 35 years. We believe every student deserves a supportive, welcoming environment where they can learn, ask questions, and grow into confident young adults.

Our school is home to 500 students and 40 dedicated teachers who care about helping every student succeed, both in the classroom and beyond.

Academic Programs

Mathematics

Our math department helps students build strong problem-solving skills.

Science

Hands-on labs in biology, chemistry, and physics let students explore how the world works.

English & Literature

Students read, write, and discuss great works while developing their own voice as writers.

Arts & Music

Painting, drama, and our award-winning school band give students room to be creative.
